About SpicyChat QoL

SpicyChat QoL

Developed under DragonScript by DragonGRaf.

An independent quality-of-life project for SpicyChat focused on optional tools, local-first data, creator helpers, chat utilities, filtering, organization, and interface cleanup.

The extension uses normal SpicyChat pages/actions or information already available to the signed-in user. It does not unlock private character fields or publish creations automatically.
